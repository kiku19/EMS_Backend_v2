# Video Tutorial Script: Creating Exams & Publishing Results

**Workflow:** Setting up an exam, entering marks, and publishing results to parents
**Audience:** School admins, exam coordinators, teachers
**Estimated Duration:** 6–7 minutes
**Screen Recording Focus:** School Dashboard → Academics module

---

## Pre-Production Notes

- Pre-create a class with students and subjects
- Have sample mark data ready for entering
- Show the parent app notification at the end (screenshot or phone mockup)

---

## Scene 1 — Introduction [0:00–0:20]

**[SCREEN: Dashboard home page]**

**Narrator:**
> "This tutorial covers the complete exam workflow — from creating an exam and entering marks to publishing results so parents can view them in the Parent App."

---

## Scene 2 — Navigate to Academics [0:20–0:40]

**[ACTION: Click "Academics" in the left sidebar]**

**Narrator:**
> "Click 'Academics' in the navigation. You'll see tabs for Exams, Results, and Report Cards."

**[SCREEN: Academics page with tabs]**

---

## Scene 3 — Create a New Exam [0:40–2:00]

**[ACTION: Click "Create Exam" button]**

**Narrator:**
> "Click 'Create Exam' to start setting up a new exam."

**[SCREEN: Create Exam form]**

> "Enter the exam name — for example, 'First Term Examination 2025'. Select the exam type: Unit Test, Mid-Term, Final, or a custom type your school has defined."

**[ACTION: Fill exam name, select type]**

> "Select which classes this exam applies to. You can assign the same exam to multiple classes."

**[ACTION: Select classes from multi-select dropdown]**

> "Set the exam date range — from start date to end date. Individual subject dates can be set in the next step."

**[ACTION: Set date range]**

> "Set the total marks and passing marks for each subject. These defaults can be overridden per subject."

**[ACTION: Enter total marks: 100, passing marks: 35]**

**[CALLOUT BOX: "Passing marks must be less than total marks"]**

> "Click 'Create Exam' to save the exam structure. It's created in Draft status — students and parents won't see it yet."

**[ACTION: Click Create Exam, show success]**

---

## Scene 4 — Enter Subject-wise Marks [2:00–3:30]

**[ACTION: Open the exam, click "Enter Marks" for a class]**

**Narrator:**
> "Now open the exam and click 'Enter Marks' for a class. You'll see the student list with columns for each subject."

**[SCREEN: Marks entry table — rows: students, columns: subjects]**

> "Click into each cell to enter the marks. Use Tab to move to the next cell quickly. If a student was absent for an exam, mark them 'AB' instead of a number."

**[ACTION: Enter marks for 4–5 students across 3 subjects, demonstrate Tab key]**

> "The system calculates the total, percentage, and grade automatically as you enter marks."

**[CALLOUT: Point to auto-calculated Total and Grade columns]**

> "If you need to enter marks later, click 'Save Draft'. Marks are saved but not published."

**[ACTION: Click Save Draft]**

---

## Scene 5 — Review and Validate [3:30–4:15]

**[ACTION: Click "Preview Results" button]**

**Narrator:**
> "Before publishing, use 'Preview Results' to review the complete result sheet. Check for any anomalies — marks above the maximum, or students with no marks entered."

**[SCREEN: Result preview table]**

> "The system highlights any validation issues in red. Fix them before publishing."

**[ACTION: Show a highlighted error, fix it]**

---

## Scene 6 — Publish Results [4:15–5:10]

**[ACTION: Click "Publish Results" button]**

**Narrator:**
> "Once all marks are correct, click 'Publish Results'. You'll be asked to confirm — publishing makes results visible to parents in the Parent App immediately."

**[SCREEN: Confirmation dialog]**

> "Click 'Confirm Publish'. The system sends push notifications and SMS to all parents with the message: 'Results for [Exam Name] are now available.'"

**[ACTION: Click Confirm, show notification sent confirmation]**

**[SCREEN: Status changes from "Draft" to "Published"]**

---

## Scene 7 — Parent View (Parent App) [5:10–5:50]

**[SCREEN: Switch to phone mockup showing Parent App]**

**Narrator:**
> "Here's what the parent sees in the Parent App after you publish. They open the Academics section, tap on the exam, and see their child's marks, grade, and rank — along with a pass or fail indicator."

**[ACTION: Scroll through the result view in parent app mockup]**

> "Parents can also download a PDF report card from this screen."

---

## Scene 8 — Recap [5:50–6:20]

**[SCREEN: Exam list showing Published status]**

**Narrator:**
> "To recap: Go to Academics, create an exam, enter marks for each class, preview for accuracy, then publish. Results are instantly visible to parents with automatic notifications."

> "Next, we'll walk through setting up the school timetable."
