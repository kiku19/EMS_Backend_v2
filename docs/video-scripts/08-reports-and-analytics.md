# Video Tutorial Script: Generating Reports & Analytics

**Workflow:** Generating attendance, fee, and academic reports for a term
**Audience:** School admins, principals, accountants
**Estimated Duration:** 5–6 minutes
**Screen Recording Focus:** School Dashboard → Reports module

---

## Pre-Production Notes

- Ensure demo account has 1–2 months of attendance, fee, and exam data
- Show both on-screen analytics and PDF/Excel export
- Demonstrate date range filtering

---

## Scene 1 — Introduction [0:00–0:20]

**[SCREEN: Dashboard home page]**

**Narrator:**
> "Reports and analytics give you a bird's-eye view of your school's performance. In this tutorial, you'll learn how to generate attendance, fee collection, and academic reports — and export them for sharing."

---

## Scene 2 — Dashboard Overview [0:20–0:55]

**[ACTION: Show the main Dashboard page]**

**Narrator:**
> "The main dashboard already shows key metrics at a glance — total students, today's attendance percentage, fees collected this month, and upcoming exam count."

**[CALLOUT: Point to summary cards on dashboard]**

> "For detailed reports, click 'Reports' in the left navigation."

**[ACTION: Click Reports in sidebar]**

**[SCREEN: Reports page with tabs: Attendance, Fees, Academics, Staff]**

---

## Scene 3 — Attendance Report [0:55–2:15]

**[ACTION: Click "Attendance" tab]**

**Narrator:**
> "The Attendance report shows attendance data by class, student, or date range. Let's generate a monthly report."

> "Select the class — for example, Grade 5. Set the date range to last month. Leave the Student filter blank to include all students."

**[ACTION: Select class, set date range]**

> "Click 'Generate Report'. The table shows each student's attendance count: present days, absent days, late days, and attendance percentage."

**[ACTION: Click Generate Report, table appears]**

**[CALLOUT: Highlight students below 75% attendance in red]**

> "Students below 75% are flagged in red — indicating they may not meet the minimum attendance requirement. This list can be shared with class teachers for follow-up."

> "Click 'Export to Excel' to download the data, or 'Export to PDF' for a printable report."

**[ACTION: Click Export to Excel, show download starts]**

---

## Scene 4 — Fee Collection Report [2:15–3:30]

**[ACTION: Click "Fees" tab]**

**Narrator:**
> "The Fees report shows collection status, outstanding balances, and payment history."

> "Select the date range and optionally filter by class or fee category. Click 'Generate'."

**[ACTION: Set date range, click Generate]**

**[SCREEN: Fee summary: Total Expected, Total Collected, Outstanding Balance]**

> "The summary shows total expected revenue versus what's been collected — and the outstanding balance. Below, you see a breakdown by class."

**[ACTION: Scroll down to class-wise breakdown]**

> "The Defaulters List shows students with unpaid fees over 30 days — click 'Export Defaulters' to get a list you can use for follow-up calls."

**[ACTION: Click Export Defaulters]**

---

## Scene 5 — Academic Performance Report [3:30–4:30]

**[ACTION: Click "Academics" tab]**

**Narrator:**
> "The Academics report shows exam performance by class or student. Select the exam — for example, 'First Term Examination' — and the class."

**[ACTION: Select exam and class]**

> "Click 'Generate'. You'll see pass/fail counts, average marks per subject, and the class rank list."

**[ACTION: Click Generate, scroll through results]**

**[CALLOUT: Point to subject-wise average bar chart]**

> "The subject-wise bar chart instantly shows which subjects the class performed best and worst in — helping teachers identify where extra attention is needed."

> "Click 'Export Report Cards' to generate individual student report card PDFs — you can print these for parent-teacher meetings."

**[ACTION: Click Export Report Cards]**

---

## Scene 6 — Staff Attendance Report [4:30–5:00]

**[ACTION: Click "Staff" tab]**

**Narrator:**
> "You can also generate a staff attendance report — useful for payroll and leave management. Select the month and click Generate."

**[ACTION: Select month, generate report]**

**[SCREEN: Staff list with present/absent/leave days]**

---

## Scene 7 — Recap [5:00–5:30]

**[SCREEN: Reports page with tabs visible]**

**Narrator:**
> "To recap: Go to Reports, select the report type — Attendance, Fees, or Academics — apply your filters, generate, and export. Reports can be downloaded as Excel or PDF for sharing with management or parents."

> "In the next video, we'll show how to use the Front Desk module for visitor management and gate passes."
