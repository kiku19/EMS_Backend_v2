# Video Tutorial Script: Fee Collection & Payment Recording

**Workflow:** Recording a fee payment from a student
**Audience:** School admins, accountants, front-desk staff
**Estimated Duration:** 5–6 minutes
**Screen Recording Focus:** School Dashboard → Fees module

---

## Pre-Production Notes

- Use a demo student who has an outstanding fee balance
- Have a sample receipt number ready if not auto-generated
- Show both full and partial payment flows

---

## Scene 1 — Introduction [0:00–0:20]

**[SCREEN: Dashboard home page]**

**Narrator:**
> "In this tutorial, you'll learn how to record a student's fee payment in EMS. Whether it's a full payment or an installment, the process takes under a minute."

---

## Scene 2 — Navigate to Fees [0:20–0:40]

**[ACTION: Click "Fees" in the left sidebar]**

**Narrator:**
> "Click 'Fees' in the left navigation. This opens the fee management section where you can see all students with their fee status."

**[SCREEN: Fees list with columns: Student, Class, Total Fee, Paid, Balance, Status]**

---

## Scene 3 — Find the Student [0:40–1:10]

**[ACTION: Use search bar or filter to find specific student]**

**Narrator:**
> "Use the search bar to find the student by name, admission number, or class. You can also filter by payment status — for example, show only students with pending dues."

**[ACTION: Type student name, result appears]**

> "Click the student's row to open their fee details."

---

## Scene 4 — Student Fee Overview [1:10–1:50]

**[SCREEN: Student fee detail page]**

**Narrator:**
> "This page shows the student's complete fee structure — broken down by fee heads such as Tuition, Transport, Exam Fee, and others defined by your school."

**[CALLOUT: Point to Total Fee, Paid Amount, Balance columns]**

> "The summary at the top shows the total amount due, how much has been paid, and the remaining balance. You can also see previous payment receipts here."

---

## Scene 5 — Record a Payment [1:50–3:30]

**[ACTION: Click "Collect Fee" or "Record Payment" button]**

**Narrator:**
> "Click 'Collect Fee' to open the payment form."

**[SCREEN: Payment form modal]**

> "Select the payment mode — Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer, Cheque, or Online. For cheque payments, you'll be prompted for the cheque number and bank name."

**[ACTION: Select "Cash" from payment mode dropdown]**

> "Enter the amount being paid. If the student is paying in full, this will match the balance. For installments, enter the partial amount."

**[ACTION: Enter payment amount]**

> "The receipt number is auto-generated. You can optionally add a note — for example, 'Q3 installment'."

**[ACTION: Enter optional note]**

> "Verify the payment date — it defaults to today. Change it if recording a backdated payment."

**[ACTION: Verify/change date]**

**[CALLOUT BOX: "Receipts are auto-numbered to prevent duplicates"]**

---

## Scene 6 — Confirm and Print Receipt [3:30–4:30]

**[ACTION: Click "Save Payment" / "Confirm" button]**

**Narrator:**
> "Click 'Save Payment'. The system records the transaction and updates the student's balance instantly."

**[SCREEN: Updated balance shown, payment appears in transaction history]**

> "A digital receipt is generated automatically. Click 'Print Receipt' to open the printable receipt PDF."

**[ACTION: Click "Print Receipt", show PDF preview]**

> "The receipt includes the school name, student name, receipt number, payment details, and authorized signature area. Hand this to the parent as proof of payment."

---

## Scene 7 — Concession / Discount [4:30–5:10]

**[ACTION: Navigate back, open a different student, show concession]**

**Narrator:**
> "If a student qualifies for a fee concession or scholarship, you can apply it before collecting. Open the student's fee page, click 'Apply Concession', enter the amount or percentage, and save."

**[ACTION: Click "Apply Concession", fill form, save]**

> "The concession reduces the total payable amount. All concession records are tracked for audit purposes."

---

## Scene 8 — Recap [5:10–5:40]

**[SCREEN: Transaction history showing payment recorded]**

**Narrator:**
> "To recap: Go to Fees, find the student, click Collect Fee, select payment mode, enter amount, and save. A receipt is generated automatically. For concessions, apply them before collecting."

> "Next, we'll walk through marking daily attendance for a class."
