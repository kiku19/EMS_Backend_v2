# Video Tutorial Script: Student Enrollment

**Workflow:** Admitting a new student into the system
**Audience:** School admins, front-desk staff
**Estimated Duration:** 4–5 minutes
**Screen Recording Focus:** School Dashboard → Students module

---

## Pre-Production Notes

- Use a demo school account with at least one class created
- Have sample student data ready (name, DOB, parent contact)
- Record at 1920×1080, zoom into form fields as you fill them

---

## Scene 1 — Introduction [0:00–0:20]

**[SCREEN: Dashboard home page]**

**Narrator:**
> "Welcome to this tutorial on enrolling a new student in the EMS school dashboard. In the next few minutes, you'll learn how to add a student, fill in their personal and academic details, and assign them to a class — all in one flow."

---

## Scene 2 — Navigate to Students [0:20–0:35]

**[ACTION: Click "Students" in the left sidebar]**

**Narrator:**
> "Start by clicking 'Students' in the left navigation menu. This takes you to the Students list page where you can see all enrolled students."

**[SCREEN: Students list page with existing students]**

---

## Scene 3 — Open Add Student Form [0:35–0:55]

**[ACTION: Click the blue "Add Student" button in the top-right corner]**

**Narrator:**
> "Click 'Add Student' to open the enrollment form. The form is divided into sections — Personal Info, Academic Details, and Parent/Guardian Information."

**[SCREEN: Add Student modal/drawer opens]**

---

## Scene 4 — Fill Personal Information [0:55–2:00]

**[ACTION: Fill in each field while narrator speaks]**

**Narrator:**
> "Start with the student's full name. Then enter their date of birth — the system will auto-calculate their age."

**[ACTION: Fill First Name, Last Name, DOB]**

> "Select the gender, then enter the Aadhaar number if available. Upload a profile photo by clicking the photo placeholder."

**[ACTION: Select gender, enter Aadhaar, upload photo]**

> "Fill in the student's address, blood group, and any medical notes that the school should be aware of."

**[ACTION: Fill address, blood group, medical notes]**

**[CALLOUT BOX: "Tip: Fields marked with * are required"]**

---

## Scene 5 — Academic Details [2:00–2:45]

**[ACTION: Scroll to Academic Details section]**

**Narrator:**
> "In the Academic Details section, assign the student to a class. Select the academic year — this defaults to the current year."

**[ACTION: Select Class from dropdown, verify academic year]**

> "Enter the admission number. If your school uses auto-generated IDs, this field will pre-fill. You can also set the admission date — this defaults to today."

**[ACTION: Set admission number, admission date]**

> "Select the student's previous school if transferring, and choose their fee category. This links the student to the correct fee structure."

**[ACTION: Select fee category]**

---

## Scene 6 — Parent/Guardian Information [2:45–3:30]

**[ACTION: Scroll to Parent section]**

**Narrator:**
> "Every student must have at least one parent or guardian linked. Fill in the father's and mother's name, occupation, phone number, and email."

**[ACTION: Fill parent details]**

> "The parent's phone number is important — it's used for SMS notifications and for the Parent App login. Make sure it's accurate."

**[CALLOUT BOX: "Parent phone = Parent App login credential"]**

---

## Scene 7 — Save and Confirm [3:30–4:10]

**[ACTION: Click "Save Student" / "Submit" button]**

**Narrator:**
> "Once you've filled all required fields, click 'Save Student'. The system validates the data and creates the student record."

**[SCREEN: Success toast notification appears]**

> "The student now appears in your Students list and is assigned to the selected class. The parent will receive an SMS with their app login details."

**[ACTION: Show student in list, click to view profile]**

---

## Scene 8 — Recap [4:10–4:40]

**[SCREEN: Student profile page showing filled details]**

**Narrator:**
> "To recap: Navigate to Students, click Add Student, fill in personal, academic, and parent details, then save. The student is now fully enrolled and ready for attendance, fee collection, and exam tracking."

> "In the next video, we'll cover how to collect fees from this student."
