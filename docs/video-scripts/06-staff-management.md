# Video Tutorial Script: Staff Onboarding & Management

**Workflow:** Adding a new staff member, assigning a role, and linking to a class
**Audience:** School admins, HR staff
**Estimated Duration:** 5–6 minutes
**Screen Recording Focus:** School Dashboard → Staff module

---

## Pre-Production Notes

- Have staff details ready: name, designation, department, salary info
- Show both teaching and non-teaching staff
- Demonstrate class teacher assignment

---

## Scene 1 — Introduction [0:00–0:20]

**[SCREEN: Dashboard home page]**

**Narrator:**
> "In this tutorial, you'll learn how to add a new staff member to EMS — whether they're a teacher, administrator, or support staff — and assign them the right role and class."

---

## Scene 2 — Navigate to Staff [0:20–0:40]

**[ACTION: Click "Staff" in the left sidebar]**

**Narrator:**
> "Click 'Staff' in the left navigation. You'll see the full list of existing staff members with their designation and department."

**[SCREEN: Staff list page]**

---

## Scene 3 — Add New Staff [0:40–1:00]

**[ACTION: Click "Add Staff" button]**

**Narrator:**
> "Click 'Add Staff' to open the staff onboarding form."

**[SCREEN: Add Staff form — Personal Info, Professional Info, Account sections]**

---

## Scene 4 — Personal Information [1:00–1:50]

**[ACTION: Fill in fields while narrating]**

**Narrator:**
> "Fill in the staff member's full name, date of birth, and gender. Upload a profile photo and enter contact details — phone number and email."

**[ACTION: Fill first name, last name, DOB, gender, phone, email]**

> "Enter their address and emergency contact information."

**[ACTION: Fill address and emergency contact]**

**[CALLOUT BOX: "Phone number is used for Staff App login"]**

---

## Scene 5 — Professional Information [1:50–3:00]

**[ACTION: Scroll to Professional Info section]**

**Narrator:**
> "In the Professional section, select their designation — Teacher, Vice Principal, Librarian, and so on. Then select their department."

**[ACTION: Select designation: Teacher, department: Science]**

> "Enter the date of joining and their employee ID. If your school uses auto-generated IDs, this pre-fills."

**[ACTION: Fill joining date, employee ID]**

> "Set their qualification — highest degree, institution, and year. This is stored for compliance and payroll purposes."

**[ACTION: Fill qualification details]**

> "For teaching staff, select which subjects they are qualified to teach. This list is used when assigning teachers to the timetable."

**[ACTION: Select subjects from multi-select: Mathematics, Physics]**

---

## Scene 6 — Salary & Payroll Setup [3:00–3:40]

**[ACTION: Scroll to Salary section]**

**Narrator:**
> "Enter the staff member's salary details — basic salary, allowances, and deductions. These are used to auto-generate monthly payslips."

**[ACTION: Fill basic salary, HRA, PF deduction]**

> "Select the salary type: Monthly, Daily Rate, or Contractual."

**[ACTION: Select Monthly]**

**[CALLOUT BOX: "Payslips are generated on the last day of each month automatically"]**

---

## Scene 7 — Save and Assign App Access [3:40–4:20]

**[ACTION: Click Save Staff button]**

**Narrator:**
> "Click 'Save Staff'. The system creates the account and sends the staff member a login link via SMS and email for the Staff App."

**[SCREEN: Success toast with "Login credentials sent"]**

> "The staff member can now log into the Staff App using their phone number as the username."

---

## Scene 8 — Assign as Class Teacher [4:20–5:00]

**[ACTION: Go to Classes → Select a class → Edit]**

**Narrator:**
> "To assign this staff member as a class teacher, go to 'Classes', open the relevant class, and click 'Edit'. In the 'Class Teacher' dropdown, select the staff member."

**[ACTION: Select the newly added teacher from Class Teacher dropdown, save]**

> "The class teacher can now view the full class roster, mark attendance, and send remarks to parents."

---

## Scene 9 — Recap [5:00–5:30]

**[SCREEN: Staff profile page of the new staff member]**

**Narrator:**
> "To recap: Go to Staff, click Add Staff, fill in personal, professional, and salary details, save — and login credentials are automatically sent. Assign them as a class teacher from the Classes module."

> "In the next video, we'll cover sending announcements and messages to parents."

---
