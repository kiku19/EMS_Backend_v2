# Video Tutorial Script: Setting Up the Class Timetable

**Workflow:** Creating and publishing a weekly timetable for a class
**Audience:** School admins, timetable coordinators
**Estimated Duration:** 5–6 minutes
**Screen Recording Focus:** School Dashboard → Classes → Timetable

---

## Pre-Production Notes

- Ensure subjects and teachers are already created in the system
- Use a class with 5–8 subjects for demonstration
- Show how teacher conflicts are detected

---

## Scene 1 — Introduction [0:00–0:20]

**[SCREEN: Dashboard home page]**

**Narrator:**
> "This tutorial shows you how to build a weekly class timetable in EMS — assigning subjects, teachers, and time slots for each day of the week."

---

## Scene 2 — Navigate to the Class [0:20–0:45]

**[ACTION: Click "Classes" → select a class]**

**Narrator:**
> "Go to 'Classes' and open the class you want to create a timetable for. Then click the 'Timetable' tab."

**[ACTION: Click Timetable tab]**

**[SCREEN: Empty timetable grid — rows: periods, columns: days of the week]**

---

## Scene 3 — Add Time Slots [0:45–1:30]

**[ACTION: Click "Configure Periods" or "Add Period"]**

**Narrator:**
> "First, configure your school's daily periods. Click 'Configure Periods' and define each period's start and end time. For example: Period 1 from 8:00 AM to 8:45 AM."

**[ACTION: Add 6 periods with start/end times]**

> "You can also add break periods — such as a 15-minute interval after Period 3. These won't show subjects and serve as visual separators."

**[ACTION: Add a break slot]**

> "Click 'Save Periods'. These time slots now apply to all days of the week."

---

## Scene 4 — Assign Subjects to Slots [1:30–3:30]

**[SCREEN: Timetable grid with days as columns, periods as rows]**

**Narrator:**
> "Now assign subjects to each slot. Click any empty cell in the grid."

**[ACTION: Click a cell, e.g., Monday / Period 1]**

> "A dropdown appears with the class's subjects. Select a subject, then select the teacher for that slot."

**[ACTION: Select 'Mathematics', then select 'Mr. Ramesh Kumar']**

> "The cell now shows the subject name and teacher. Continue filling in the rest of the week."

**[ACTION: Fill in several more cells across different days]**

**[CALLOUT BOX: "Each teacher can only be assigned to one class at the same time slot"]**

---

## Scene 5 — Conflict Detection [3:30–4:00]

**[ACTION: Assign the same teacher to two overlapping slots]**

**Narrator:**
> "If you accidentally assign a teacher who is already scheduled in another class at the same time, EMS will warn you immediately."

**[SCREEN: Red warning: "Mr. Ramesh Kumar is already assigned to Grade 4-A at this time"]**

> "The system prevents double-booking — ensuring no teacher is split across two classes simultaneously."

**[ACTION: Change to a different teacher, warning clears]**

---

## Scene 6 — Copy Timetable to Another Day [4:00–4:30]

**[ACTION: Right-click Monday column or use "Copy Day" option]**

**Narrator:**
> "If your schedule repeats — for example, Monday and Wednesday have the same periods — use 'Copy Day'. Select Monday as the source and Wednesday as the destination."

**[ACTION: Use Copy Day, show Wednesday fills automatically]**

---

## Scene 7 — Publish the Timetable [4:30–5:00]

**[ACTION: Click "Publish Timetable" button]**

**Narrator:**
> "When the timetable is complete, click 'Publish'. This makes it visible to teachers in their dashboards and to students and parents in the respective apps."

**[SCREEN: Success confirmation, timetable status: Published]**

> "Teachers will see their personal timetable in the Staff App — showing which class they teach each period."

---

## Scene 8 — Recap [5:00–5:30]

**[SCREEN: Completed timetable grid]**

**Narrator:**
> "To recap: Open a class, go to the Timetable tab, configure your daily periods, assign subjects and teachers to each slot — the system prevents scheduling conflicts — then publish."

> "In the next video, we'll cover adding and managing staff members."

---
