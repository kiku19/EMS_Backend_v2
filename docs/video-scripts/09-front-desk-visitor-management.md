# Video Tutorial Script: Front Desk — Visitor Management & Gate Passes

**Workflow:** Logging a visitor, issuing a gate pass, and managing student pickups
**Audience:** Front-desk staff, security personnel, school admins
**Estimated Duration:** 4–5 minutes
**Screen Recording Focus:** School Dashboard → Front Desk module

---

## Pre-Production Notes

- Show both visitor log entry and gate pass issuance
- Demonstrate the student pickup authorization flow
- Keep the flow concise — front-desk staff need quick reference

---

## Scene 1 — Introduction [0:00–0:20]

**[SCREEN: Dashboard home page]**

**Narrator:**
> "The Front Desk module is your digital visitor logbook and gate pass system. In this tutorial, you'll see how to log incoming visitors, issue gate passes, and manage student pickup authorizations."

---

## Scene 2 — Navigate to Front Desk [0:20–0:40]

**[ACTION: Click "Front Desk" in the left sidebar]**

**Narrator:**
> "Click 'Front Desk' in the navigation. You'll see three sections: Visitors, Gate Passes, and Pickup Requests."

**[SCREEN: Front Desk landing page with three sections]**

---

## Scene 3 — Log a New Visitor [0:40–2:00]

**[ACTION: Click "Visitors" tab → Click "Add Visitor"]**

**Narrator:**
> "When a visitor arrives, click 'Add Visitor' to log their entry."

**[SCREEN: Add Visitor form]**

> "Enter the visitor's name, phone number, and purpose of visit — for example, 'Meeting with Principal' or 'Parent enquiry'."

**[ACTION: Fill visitor name, phone, purpose]**

> "Select who they're visiting — you can search by staff name or type 'Front Desk' for general visitors."

**[ACTION: Select staff member from search]**

> "Capture their ID proof — you can type the ID number or use the camera to scan. This is optional but recommended for security."

**[ACTION: Enter Aadhaar number]**

> "Click 'Check In'. The system logs their entry time automatically."

**[ACTION: Click Check In, visitor appears in active visitors list]**

**[SCREEN: Active visitor entry with name, purpose, time-in, and status "In School"]**

---

## Scene 4 — Check Out a Visitor [2:00–2:30]

**[ACTION: Find the visitor in the Active Visitors list, click Check Out]**

**Narrator:**
> "When the visitor leaves, find their entry in the Active Visitors list and click 'Check Out'. The system logs their exit time and moves the entry to the completed log."

**[ACTION: Click Check Out, time-out is recorded]**

**[CALLOUT BOX: "All visitor logs are retained for 1 year"]**

---

## Scene 5 — Issue a Gate Pass [2:30–3:30]

**[ACTION: Click "Gate Passes" tab → Click "New Gate Pass"]**

**Narrator:**
> "Gate passes are used when a student needs to leave school early. Click 'New Gate Pass'."

**[SCREEN: Gate Pass form]**

> "Search for the student by name or admission number. Select the reason — Medical, Family Emergency, Authorized Pickup, or Other."

**[ACTION: Search and select student, choose reason]**

> "Enter the authorized person's name and phone — who will be picking the student up. Set the expected return or out time."

**[ACTION: Fill pickup person details]**

> "Click 'Issue Pass'. A printable gate pass is generated with a QR code that security can scan at the gate."

**[ACTION: Click Issue Pass, show QR-coded gate pass preview]**

> "Click 'Print' to print it out for the student."

**[ACTION: Click Print]**

---

## Scene 6 — Pickup Authorization [3:30–4:10]

**[ACTION: Navigate to "Pickup Requests" tab]**

**Narrator:**
> "If a parent requests a student pickup through the Parent App, the request appears here in the Pickup Requests tab for staff to approve."

**[SCREEN: Pickup request list with parent name, student, and request time]**

> "Click 'Approve' to authorize the pickup — the parent is notified immediately that they can collect the student."

**[ACTION: Click Approve on a request, notification sent confirmation]**

---

## Scene 7 — Recap [4:10–4:40]

**[SCREEN: Front Desk overview page]**

**Narrator:**
> "To recap: Use the Visitors tab to log and check out school visitors. Use Gate Passes to issue authorized early-exit passes for students. Use Pickup Requests to approve parent pickup requests from the app."

> "In the final video, we'll cover school settings and initial configuration."
