# Video Tutorial Script: Sending Messages & Announcements

**Workflow:** Sending a school-wide announcement and a direct message to a parent
**Audience:** School admins, class teachers, front-desk staff
**Estimated Duration:** 4–5 minutes
**Screen Recording Focus:** School Dashboard → Messaging module

---

## Pre-Production Notes

- Have at least one student with a linked parent in the demo account
- Show both broadcast announcements and 1-on-1 chat
- Use a realistic announcement scenario (e.g., school holiday notice)

---

## Scene 1 — Introduction [0:00–0:20]

**[SCREEN: Dashboard home page]**

**Narrator:**
> "Effective communication with parents is essential. In this tutorial, you'll learn how to send school-wide announcements and have direct conversations with individual parents — all from the EMS dashboard."

---

## Scene 2 — Navigate to Messaging [0:20–0:40]

**[ACTION: Click "Messaging" in the left sidebar]**

**Narrator:**
> "Click 'Messaging' in the navigation. You'll see two main sections: Announcements for broadcast messages, and Chats for direct messaging."

**[SCREEN: Messaging page with Announcements and Chats tabs]**

---

## Scene 3 — Create an Announcement [0:40–2:15]

**[ACTION: Click "Announcements" tab → Click "New Announcement"]**

**Narrator:**
> "Click 'New Announcement' to create a broadcast message."

**[SCREEN: Announcement compose form]**

> "Enter the announcement title — for example, 'School Closed on March 25th'."

**[ACTION: Type title]**

> "Write the message body. You can use the formatting toolbar to bold important details, add bullet points, or insert links."

**[ACTION: Type a school holiday notice with formatting]**

> "Choose your audience: All Parents, a specific Class, or a specific Grade level. For school-wide notices, select 'All Parents'."

**[ACTION: Select "All Parents" from audience dropdown]**

**[CALLOUT BOX: "Targeted announcements reach only the relevant parents"]**

> "Attach files if needed — for example, a holiday schedule PDF."

**[ACTION: Attach a PDF file]**

> "Set a delivery time. You can send now or schedule it for a specific date and time — useful for sending reminders the evening before an event."

**[ACTION: Show schedule option, set to "Send Now"]**

---

## Scene 4 — Send and Track Delivery [2:15–2:50]

**[ACTION: Click "Send Announcement"]**

**Narrator:**
> "Click 'Send Announcement'. The message is delivered as a push notification to all parent phones, and also appears in their Parent App notification feed."

**[SCREEN: Announcement appears in list with "Sent" status and delivery stats]**

> "Click the announcement to see delivery statistics — how many parents received it and how many have read it."

**[ACTION: Click the announcement, show Read/Delivered counts]**

---

## Scene 5 — Direct Chat with a Parent [2:50–4:00]

**[ACTION: Click "Chats" tab → Click "New Chat"]**

**Narrator:**
> "For individual conversations, use the Chats section. Click 'New Chat' and search for a parent by student name or phone number."

**[ACTION: Search for parent, select from results]**

**[SCREEN: Chat window opens]**

> "Type your message and press send. The parent receives it instantly in the Parent App's chat screen. Replies appear here in real time."

**[ACTION: Type a message, send it]**

**[CALLOUT BOX: "Chats are retained for 12 months for compliance"]**

> "You can also attach documents — such as a medical form or consent letter — directly in the chat."

**[ACTION: Click attachment icon, show file attach]**

---

## Scene 6 — Recap [4:00–4:30]

**[SCREEN: Messaging page showing both announcements and chats]**

**Narrator:**
> "To recap: For school-wide communication, go to Messaging → Announcements → New Announcement, compose your message, select the audience, and send. For direct parent communication, use the Chats tab."

> "In the next video, we'll walk through generating attendance and fee reports."
