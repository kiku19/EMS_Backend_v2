# Video Tutorial Script: Marking Class Attendance

**Workflow:** Marking daily attendance for a class
**Audience:** Teachers, class teachers, school admins
**Estimated Duration:** 4–5 minutes
**Screen Recording Focus:** School Dashboard → Classes → Attendance

---

## Pre-Production Notes

- Use a demo class with 10–15 students populated
- Show both dashboard and staff app flows if time permits
- Demonstrate both present/absent toggling and bulk mark

---

## Scene 1 — Introduction [0:00–0:20]

**[SCREEN: Dashboard home page]**

**Narrator:**
> "Attendance tracking is a daily task in EMS. In this tutorial, you'll see how to mark attendance for an entire class in under two minutes — and how absent students trigger automatic parent notifications."

---

## Scene 2 — Navigate to Classes [0:20–0:40]

**[ACTION: Click "Classes" in the left sidebar]**

**Narrator:**
> "Click 'Classes' in the navigation menu. Select the class you want to mark attendance for."

**[ACTION: Click on a specific class, e.g., "Grade 5 - Section A"]**

---

## Scene 3 — Open Attendance Tab [0:40–1:00]

**[ACTION: Click the "Attendance" tab inside the class view]**

**Narrator:**
> "Inside the class page, click the 'Attendance' tab. This shows you the attendance calendar and the student list."

**[SCREEN: Attendance tab with date picker and student list]**

---

## Scene 4 — Select Date [1:00–1:20]

**[ACTION: Verify date is today, or change if backdating]**

**Narrator:**
> "The date defaults to today. If you're recording attendance for a previous date — for example, if there was no internet yesterday — click the date picker and select the correct date."

**[ACTION: Show date picker, select today]**

---

## Scene 5 — Mark Attendance [1:20–2:45]

**[SCREEN: Student list with Present/Absent toggle for each student]**

**Narrator:**
> "All students default to 'Present'. To mark a student absent, click the toggle next to their name — it switches to red."

**[ACTION: Toggle 2–3 students to Absent]**

> "You can also mark a student as 'Late' by clicking the toggle a second time. The third click cycles back to Present."

**[ACTION: Show Late status on one student]**

> "If most students are absent — for example, during a field trip — use 'Mark All Absent' at the top, then selectively mark the present students."

**[ACTION: Click "Mark All Absent", then toggle a few to Present]**

**[CALLOUT BOX: "Tip: Use bulk actions for large classes to save time"]**

---

## Scene 6 — Add Remarks [2:45–3:15]

**[ACTION: Click the comment icon next to an absent student]**

**Narrator:**
> "For absent students, you can add a note — for example, 'Medical leave, doctor's certificate submitted'. Click the note icon next to the student's name."

**[ACTION: Type note and save]**

> "These remarks are visible to admins and can be included in attendance reports."

---

## Scene 7 — Save Attendance [3:15–3:45]

**[ACTION: Click "Save Attendance" button]**

**Narrator:**
> "Once you've marked all students, click 'Save Attendance'. The system saves the record and automatically sends SMS notifications to parents of absent students."

**[SCREEN: Success toast: "Attendance saved. 3 absence notifications sent."]**

---

## Scene 8 — View Attendance History [3:45–4:20]

**[ACTION: Click on a previous date in the calendar view]**

**Narrator:**
> "To view past attendance, click any date on the calendar. Green dates mean full attendance was recorded. You can edit past attendance if corrections are needed — the system logs who made the change."

**[SCREEN: Calendar with color-coded dates]**

---

## Scene 9 — Recap [4:20–4:50]

**[SCREEN: Attendance calendar showing the current month]**

**Narrator:**
> "To recap: Go to Classes, select a class, open the Attendance tab, mark present and absent students, add notes if needed, then save. Parents of absent students are notified automatically."

> "In the next video, we'll cover how to create and publish exam results."
