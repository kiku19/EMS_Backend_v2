# Parent App User Guide — EMS

This guide explains how to use the **EMS Parent App** on your smartphone to stay connected with your child's school.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Downloading the App
- **Android:** Search "EMS Parent" on the Google Play Store and install.
- **iOS:** Search "EMS Parent" on the Apple App Store and install.

> If your school uses a custom-branded version of the app, use the name provided by the school.

### 1.2 Creating Your Account
Your school will send you an **invitation SMS or email** with a link to register. Follow the link to set your password.

If you did not receive an invitation:
1. Open the app and tap **Sign Up**.
2. Enter the **mobile number or email** registered with the school.
3. Enter the **Student Admission Number** of your child.
4. Set a password and tap **Register**.

### 1.3 Logging In
1. Open the app.
2. Enter your **email or mobile number** and **password**.
3. Tap **Sign In**.

### 1.4 Linking Multiple Children
If you have more than one child at the same school:
- After logging in, tap the **child selector** at the top of the screen.
- Tap **+ Add Child** and enter the additional admission number.

If your children are at **different schools**, you will need a separate account for each school's app.

---

## 2. Home Screen

The home screen shows a summary for the selected child:

| Card | What it shows |
|------|--------------|
| Today's Attendance | Present / Absent status for today |
| Upcoming Exams | Next exam in the schedule |
| Fee Due | Outstanding fee balance |
| Recent Remarks | Latest remarks from teachers |
| Announcements | School-wide notices |

Tap any card to view full details.

---

## 3. Attendance

Go to **Attendance** in the bottom navigation bar:
- View your child's attendance for the current month in a calendar view.
- Green = Present, Red = Absent, Orange = Late, Grey = Holiday.
- Tap any date to see the detailed status.
- At the bottom, a summary shows the attendance percentage for the current academic year.

> If you believe an attendance entry is incorrect, contact the class teacher directly via the **Chat** feature.

---

## 4. Exam Results

Go to **Academics** → **Results**:
- View published exam results for your child.
- See marks, grades, and rank for each subject.
- Results are only visible **after the school publishes them**.

### 4.1 Report Cards
If your school uses CBSE or ICSE report cards:
- Go to **Academics** → **Report Card**.
- Download the PDF report card for the latest term.

---

## 5. Fees

Go to **Fees** in the bottom navigation bar:

### 5.1 Viewing Dues
- See the total outstanding balance.
- View a breakdown by fee head (tuition, transport, etc.).
- Each fee item shows the due amount, due date, and status.

### 5.2 Payment History
Scroll down to see a list of all past payments with receipt numbers and dates.

### 5.3 Online Payment
If your school has enabled online payments:
1. Tap **Pay Now** on the Fees screen.
2. Select the fee items you want to pay.
3. Choose a payment method (UPI, card, net banking).
4. Complete the payment — a receipt is generated automatically.

### 5.4 Downloading a Receipt
Tap any payment in the history list and tap **Download Receipt** to save a PDF.

---

## 6. Homework

Go to **Homework** in the navigation:
- See all pending and completed homework assignments.
- Each item shows the subject, due date, and description.
- Tap an assignment to read the full instructions and download attachments.

> Submission through the app depends on your school's configuration. Some schools require physical submission only.

---

## 7. Timetable

Go to **Timetable**:
- View your child's weekly class schedule.
- See subject, teacher name, and room for each period.
- Timetable changes (substitutions) are highlighted.

---

## 8. Remarks

Go to **Remarks**:
- Teachers send remarks directly to parents for behavioural, academic, health, or general updates.
- Each remark shows the teacher's name, date, type, and message.
- You will receive a **push notification** when a new remark is sent.

---

## 9. Announcements

Go to **Announcements**:
- School-wide notices from the administration.
- Includes event reminders, holiday notices, and general information.
- Tap any announcement to read the full text and any attached files.

---

## 10. Messaging (Chat)

Go to **Chat** to communicate with teachers and school staff:
1. Tap **+ New Chat** (or the compose icon).
2. Select the teacher or staff member.
3. Type your message and tap **Send**.

> Chat is only available with staff members your school has enabled for parent messaging.

---

## 11. PTM (Parent-Teacher Meeting) Booking

Go to **PTM**:
- See upcoming parent-teacher meeting sessions.
- Tap a session to view available time slots.
- Tap a slot and tap **Book** to reserve your appointment.
- You will receive a confirmation notification.

To cancel a booked slot, tap the booking and tap **Cancel**.

---

## 12. Notifications

All important updates (attendance, results, fee reminders, remarks) are sent as **push notifications**. Ensure notifications are enabled for the app in your phone settings.

Go to **Settings** → **Notifications** inside the app to configure which alerts you receive.

---

## 13. Profile & Settings

Tap your avatar (top-right corner):
- **My Profile** — update your name, email, and profile photo.
- **Change Password** — update your login password.
- **Language** — switch the app language.
- **Linked Children** — add or remove linked student profiles.
- **Notification Preferences** — control which notification types you receive.

---

## 14. Common Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Can't log in | Check you're using the email/mobile registered with the school. Use Forgot Password if needed. |
| Child not showing after login | Contact the school to verify your mobile/email is linked to the student record |
| Results not visible | Results are published by the school — they may not be available yet |
| Fees showing wrong amount | Contact the school accountant — the app reflects the school's records exactly |
| Not receiving notifications | Go to phone Settings → Apps → EMS Parent → Notifications and enable them |
| Chat not working | Some schools restrict chat to specific staff; contact the school for guidance |

---

## 15. Privacy & Data

- The app only displays data for your linked child(ren).
- Your login credentials are encrypted and never shared.
- You can request data deletion by contacting your school or emailing the EMS support team.
- For full details, see the **Privacy Policy** in the app footer.

---

## 16. Getting Help

- Tap the **?** icon in the app for in-app help.
- Contact your school directly for school-specific questions.
- For app technical issues, use the **Report a Bug** option in Settings.
