# Teacher User Guide — EMS School Dashboard

This guide covers every feature available to teachers in the EMS School Dashboard. It assumes you have already received your login credentials from your school administrator.

---

## 1. Logging In

1. Open your browser and go to your school's EMS dashboard URL.
2. Enter your **email** and **password**, then click **Sign In**.
3. If you have forgotten your password, click **Forgot Password** and enter your email — a reset link will be sent.

> Your account is tied to a single school. If you are assigned to multiple schools, each school will provide separate credentials.

---

## 2. Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you see the main dashboard with:

| Widget | What it shows |
|--------|--------------|
| My Classes Today | Your timetable for the current day |
| Pending Homework | Assignments due for grading |
| Upcoming Exams | Exams scheduled in the next 7 days |
| Attendance Summary | Your classes' attendance rates this week |
| Recent Announcements | School-wide notices from the admin |

---

## 3. My Classes

### 3.1 Viewing Your Assigned Classes
Go to **Classes** in the left sidebar. You will see only the classes you are assigned to teach.

### 3.2 Class Details
Click any class card to open the class detail panel:
- **Students tab** — full roster with photos, admission numbers, and contact info
- **Attendance tab** — mark or view attendance for any date
- **Timetable tab** — your weekly schedule for this class
- **Homework tab** — assignments given to this class

---

## 4. Attendance

### 4.1 Marking Attendance
1. Go to **Classes** → select a class → **Attendance** tab.
2. Choose the **date** (defaults to today).
3. For each student, click:
   - **P** — Present
   - **A** — Absent
   - **L** — Late
   - **E** — Excused
4. Click **Save Attendance**.

> Attendance can only be submitted once per class per day. Contact your admin to correct an already-saved record.

### 4.2 Viewing Attendance History
Use the calendar picker to navigate to past dates. A colour-coded summary shows present/absent/late counts.

### 4.3 Bulk Attendance (Mark All Present)
Click **Mark All Present** at the top of the attendance list, then adjust individual exceptions before saving.

---

## 5. Homework

### 5.1 Creating a Homework Assignment
1. Go to **Homework** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ Add Homework**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Title** — assignment name
   - **Class & Subject** — which class and subject this is for
   - **Due Date** — deadline for submission
   - **Description** — instructions for students
   - **Attachment** — optional file (PDF, image)
4. Click **Create**.

### 5.2 Grading Submissions
1. Click the **eye icon** (👁) on any homework card to open the detail modal.
2. You will see a table of students with their submission status.
3. For each submitted student, enter:
   - **Grade / Marks**
   - **Remarks** (optional feedback)
4. To mark a paper-based submission, click **Mark as Submitted** next to the student's name.
5. Click **Save Grades** when done.

### 5.3 Editing or Deleting Homework
Click the **three-dot menu** (⋮) on a homework card and select **Edit** or **Delete**.

---

## 6. Timetable

### 6.1 Viewing Your Timetable
Go to **Timetable** in the sidebar to see your complete weekly schedule — subject, class, and room for each period.

### 6.2 Substitution Alerts
If you are assigned as a substitute for another teacher, you will see a notification badge on the Timetable page. The substitution period is highlighted in orange.

---

## 7. Exams

### 7.1 Viewing Upcoming Exams
Go to **Academics** → **Exams** to see all upcoming exams for your classes with dates, subjects, and venues.

### 7.2 Entering Results
1. Go to **Academics** → **Results**.
2. Select the exam and class.
3. Enter marks for each student.
4. Click **Save Results**.

> Results are in **draft** state until an admin publishes them. Parents will not see results until they are published.

### 7.3 CBSE Report Cards
If your school follows CBSE, go to **Academics** → **CBSE Report Card** to view generated report cards for students in your classes.

---

## 8. Remarks

### 8.1 Writing a Remark
1. Go to **Students** → search for the student.
2. Open the student profile and click the **Remarks** tab.
3. Click **+ Add Remark**.
4. Select the **type** (Behavioural, Academic, Health, etc.).
5. Write your remark and toggle **Send to Parent** if the parent should see it.
6. Click **Save**.

> Remarks marked "Send to Parent" appear in the parent's mobile app instantly.

---

## 9. Messaging

### 9.1 Sending a Message
Go to **Messaging** → **Chat** to send direct messages to other staff members or to parents.

### 9.2 Announcements
Announcements are read-only for teachers unless your admin has granted you announcement creation rights.

---

## 10. PTM (Parent-Teacher Meeting)

### 10.1 Viewing PTM Sessions
Go to **PTM** in the sidebar to see all scheduled parent-teacher meeting sessions.

### 10.2 Viewing Booked Slots
Click a PTM session card to see which parents have booked time slots with you.

---

## 11. Leaves

Go to **Staffs** → **My Leaves** (or the Leaves link in the top profile menu) to:
- Apply for a leave with a reason and date range.
- View your leave history and approval status.

---

## 12. Profile & Settings

Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner:
- **Profile** — update your name, phone, and profile photo.
- **Change Password** — update your password.
- **Language** — switch the dashboard language.

---

## 13. Common Issues

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Can't see a class in my list | Ask your admin to assign you to the class in Staff settings |
| Attendance already submitted error | Contact admin — attendance corrections require admin access |
| Student not showing in roster | The student may not be enrolled in that class yet |
| Results not visible to parents | An admin must publish the exam results first |
| Can't access a module | Your role may not have permission — ask your admin to update your permissions |

---

## 14. Getting Help

- Use the **?** (help) icon next to any section heading for context-sensitive guidance.
- Search the **Knowledge Base** from the Help menu for articles and how-to guides.
- Contact your school administrator for account or permission issues.
